# app-dev

## Overview

**Jujutsu Kaisen** is a dark-fantasy action anime and manga about sorcerers who fight curses — evil energies born from negative human emotions. The story follows Yuji Itadori, a kind-hearted high schooler who becomes involved with the world of jujutsu after swallowing a cursed object.

## Main Characters

- **Yuji Itadori** — The main hero; strong, fast, and compassionate.
- **Megumi Fushiguro** — Stoic sorcerer who uses shadowy shikigami.
- **Nobara Kugisaki** — Confident sorcerer who uses nails and a hammer.
- **Satoru Gojo** — Extremely powerful teacher with special eyes and space-bending techniques.
- **Sukuna** — The "King of Curses", a powerful spirit sealed inside Yuji.
